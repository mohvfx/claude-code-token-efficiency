# Attribution and provenance

## Content

The guidance in this repository is **derived from a public Anthropic workshop**, *"Maximizing the value of Claude
Code"* (Claude Code Workshop), roughly 26 minutes long. It was transcribed end to end, then restructured into a
procedure: a diagnostic table, checklists, references, and an audit script.

What that means in practice:

- This repository contains **summaries, paraphrases and code**, not the workshop itself.
- The **source video is not redistributed here**, and neither is the full transcript.
- Anything I added on top of the workshop is marked as such in `references/source-notes.md`. In short:
  `scripts/audit.py`, the severity levels, the 1,200 / 3,000 token thresholds, and the diagnostic table structure are
  my own work, not Anthropic's.
- Model behaviour, pricing and product details change. Figures quoted here are dated estimates, not specifications.
